Overview

Developed the Flutter and Android applications powering the advertising-robot platform.

Built Flutter and Android operator interfaces for autonomous advertising robots, with ROS1 & ROS 2 integration, real-time communication, live telemetry, and low-latency control. The platform has 100+ robots across 45+ sites in India and the UAE.

Contribution

Developed Flutter and Android applications for the advertising-robot platform, contributed to the Flutter–ROS bridge, integrated multi-protocol communication (WebSockets, UART, WebRTC), improved performance and stability across versions, and supported commercial deployments in India and the UAE.
